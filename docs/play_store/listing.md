## Full Description

Transform your receipt management with YNAB Receipt Scanner—the fastest way to track expenses in You Need A Budget.

**📸 Instant Capture**
Point your camera at any receipt and instantly extract key details: payee, amount, and date. Our advanced OCR technology reads receipts accurately, so you don't have to type.

**🔄 Auto-Sync to YNAB**
Seamlessly connect your YNAB account and sync transactions with one tap. Edit details before submitting to ensure perfect accuracy. Your budget stays up-to-date effortlessly.

**💾 Receipt Storage**
Keep digital copies of all your receipts organized in one place. Never lose a receipt again—they're backed up in your YNAB account.

**✨ Key Features**
• Smart receipt scanning with OCR
• Direct integration with YNAB API
• Automatic transaction creation
• Offline mode—scan now, sync later
• Multiple account support
• Category assignment
• Receipt history and search
• Dark mode support
• Privacy-focused—all data stays local or in your YNAB account

**🔒 Secure & Private**
Your financial data is yours alone. We don't store receipts on our servers, and we never share your information with third parties. All communication with YNAB uses secure OAuth authentication.

**🌍 Multi-Language Support**
Available in English and Spanish, with more languages coming soon.

**🎯 Perfect For**
• YNAB users who want faster transaction entry
• People who need receipt records for taxes
• Anyone tracking business expenses
• Families managing household budgets

**Why Choose YNAB Receipt Scanner?**
✅ Saves time—no more manual entry
✅ Increases accuracy—OCR extracts exact amounts
✅ Improves compliance—digital receipt archive
✅ Completely free and open source

**Requirements**

• Camera permission for scanning
